How to Cite ReelWorld Plates

When referencing visual records in research papers, exhibition monographs, or municipal publications, adhere to the standard open cadastre citation format to honor creator lineage and preserve provenance:

Photo: [Photographer Name] / ReelWorld Open Cadastre ([License Code]) • https://cadastre.reelworld.org/plate/[PLATE-ID]
Example: Photo: C. Karanja / ReelWorld Open Cadastre (CC BY-SA 4.0) • https://cadastre.reelworld.org/plate/NBO-0021
