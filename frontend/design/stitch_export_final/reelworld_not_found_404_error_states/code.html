System Epistemology

Our Error Handling Philosophy

Museum curation requires complete institutional transparency. We treat infrastructure fault lines with mathematical candor rather than decorative obfuscation.

Honest Failure

Reel tells you when it is not sure, and states precisely what could not be found. We never conceal algorithmic ambiguity or system dropouts behind artificial cheerfulness or user blame.

Zero Telemetry Leaks

System errors generate anonymous stack signals only. We never record your search strings, cadastre coordinates, uncommitted photography, or local device fingerprints to central logs.
