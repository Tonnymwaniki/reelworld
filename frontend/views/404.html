System Epistemology

Our Error Handling Philosophy

Museum curation requires complete institutional transparency. We treat fault lines with candor rather than decorative obfuscation.

Principle 01

Honest Failure

Reel tells you when it is not sure, and states precisely what could not be found. We never conceal ambiguity behind artificial cheerfulness or user blame.

Principle 02

Zero Telemetry Leaks

System errors generate no records about you. We never log your search strings, photos, or device fingerprints.

Preserved Sandboxes

A disrupted connection never purges your workspace. Your comparisons and drafts stay in local memory, ready to continue.
